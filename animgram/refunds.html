Refund policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

14 days, no questions asked

If AnimGram is not for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase and you will get your money back in full. You do not need to give a reason.

How to ask

Email jwi@j-w-i.org from the address you bought with, or include your order number. Payments are handled by our payment provider, which acts as the merchant of record, so refunds are processed through the payment provider and returned to your original payment method. It can take several business days to appear. After a refund, the licence to use the app ends.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

This policy is in addition to, and does not limit, your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. If the app has a major failure, you are entitled to a replacement or a refund, and to compensation for other reasonably foreseeable loss, even after the 14 days. Those rights cannot be excluded by us.

Early access

AnimGram is early access and may have bugs. A bug that you tell us about is not a reason to wait: you can ask for your refund at any time in the first 14 days.
